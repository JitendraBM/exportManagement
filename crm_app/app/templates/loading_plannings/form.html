Packing → containers — assign each numbered packing

The table lists every packing for reference. To load a container, type its packings into the box under it — unique packing ids or Loading no.s, separated by commas or new lines, and A - B for a range, which means every packing between A and B in the order listed above (not arithmetic on the id). Fill in as many boxes as you like, then press Apply all. An id left out of every box is unassigned; an id in two boxes stays with the first and is flagged. Auto-assign spreads the packings evenly across the containers by weight as a starting point, filling the boxes for you to adjust. Packing no. and the unique packing id are never reissued here, so a row matches the label physically on the pallet.

Container summary — VGM check

Packings are assigned whole. VGM is every packing's gross plus the container's own tare; it turns red past the max permitted weight, which is a warning only — the plan still saves.
